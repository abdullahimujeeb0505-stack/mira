## Answer

**Early Life & Education** [SRC-755242fd3ce6]
- Elon Reeve Musk was born on 28 June 1971 in Pretoria, South Africa, and grew up with siblings Kimbal and Tosca. [SRC-755242fd3ce6]
His parents are Maye Musk and Errol Musk, who divorced when he was ten [ SRC-755242fd3ce6 ].
- As a teenager he taught himself computer programming and sold a simple video game, Blastar, at age 12 [ SRC-755242fd3ce6 ].
- He moved to Canada in 1989 [ SRC-3a72702043ff ] and later attended university in the United States, studying physics and economics before pursuing entrepreneurial ventures.

**Early Ventures** [SRC-755242fd3ce6]
- In 1995 Musk co‑founded Zip2, an online city‑guide software company [ SRC-3a72702043ff ].
- In 1999 he founded X.com, an online payment company that later became PayPal [ SRC-3a72702043ff ].

**SpaceX** [SRC-c8475b2dac28]
- Musk founded Space Exploration Technologies Corp. (SpaceX) in May 2002 [ SRC-3a72702043ff ].
- Key milestones include the first Falcon 1 orbital flight on 28 September 2008 [ SRC-3a72702043ff ], the first Dragon spacecraft docking with the ISS on 22 May 2012 [ SRC-3a72702043ff ], and the first crewed Dragon launch on 30 May 2020 [ SRC-3a72702043ff ].

**Tesla** [SRC-c8475b2dac28]
- Musk joined Tesla Motors in February 2004 [ SRC-3a72702043ff ].
- Tesla went public on 29 June 2010 [ SRC-3a72702043ff ].
- He has been described as the CEO of Tesla, but the exact date he assumed the CEO title is not specified in the retrieved sources (unverified: no matching retrieved evidence).

**Other Ventures** [SRC-3a72702043ff]
- In 2016 Musk launched Neuralink and The Boring Company, focusing on brain‑computer interfaces and tunnelling infrastructure respectively. [SRC-c8475b2dac28]
- He acquired the social‑media platform X (formerly Twitter) in October 2022. [SRC-c8475b2dac28]
- In March 2023 he founded xAI, an artificial‑intelligence research company [ SRC-3a72702043ff ].

**Recent Highlights**
- In May 2018 Musk was elected a Fellow of the Royal Society [ SRC-3a72702043ff ].
- A Starship booster was successfully caught on 13 October 2024 [ SRC-3a72702043ff ].
