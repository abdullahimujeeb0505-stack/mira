**Name**: The project is being presented as a next‑generation Tesla Roadster equipped with a SpaceX‑developed thruster package (often described as a “Tesla Roadster flying car”). [SRC-e54bf1734474]

**Development status**: A prototype performed aerial maneuvers at a SpaceX test site on 15 Oct 2026, showing the ability to drive up a curved ramp, flip upside‑down, fire thrusters, and land on its wheels[SRC-2e795e58ccf9].
Tesla and SpaceX have said production is targeted for 2028, but the vehicle is still in the announced/pre‑production phase and not yet for sale[SRC-0ffa6a685815].

**Key specifications (as reported)**:
- Powertrain: tri‑motor AWD, 0‑60 mph in ~1.9 s (sub‑1 s with the SpaceX package claimed)[SRC-0ffa6a685815]
- Range: up to 620 mi (EPA‑range claim)[SRC-0ffa6a685815]
- Top speed: >250 mph[SRC-0ffa6a685815]
- Thruster system: ultra‑high‑pressure air (cold‑gas) thrusters supplied by SpaceX, used for short thrust bursts that enable brief aerial lifts and mid‑air rotation (unverified: no matching retrieved evidence).
- Additional hardware: suction system beneath the car to maintain surface contact during upside‑down maneuvers[SRC-2e795e58ccf9]

**Intended use / positioning**: Musk described the demonstration as proof that “flying cars are real” and suggested the vehicle could be a street‑legal car capable of lifting off the ground, but officials emphasized that the aerial demos do not imply readiness for unrestricted public use (unverified: no matching retrieved evidence).

**Official statements / credible reports**: Elon Musk publicly said the Roadster “can fly” during the livestream and highlighted the collaboration with SpaceX on the thruster system[SRC-2e795e58ccf9].
Coverage by Blueprint Newspapers and M9.news reported the demonstration and the 2028 production target, noting that safety, regulatory approval, and full‑flight capability remain unresolved[SRC-2e795e58ccf9].

**Current gaps**: No detailed technical data (thrust, flight duration, altitude, or redundancy systems) have been released, and no regulatory agency (FAA, EASA) has confirmed certification pathways (unverified: no matching retrieved evidence).
Pricing beyond the reservation range ($50‑250 k reported elsewhere) has not been disclosed (unverified: no matching retrieved evidence).

**Summary**: The Tesla Roadster “flying car” is a prototype‑level vehicle that combines a high‑performance electric sports car with SpaceX cold‑gas thrusters for short, dramatic aerial maneuvers. [SRC-2e795e58ccf9]
