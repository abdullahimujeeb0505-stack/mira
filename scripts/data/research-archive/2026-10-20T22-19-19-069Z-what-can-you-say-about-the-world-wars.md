- What were the primary causes and outcomes of World War I?
- What were the primary causes and outcomes of World War II?

## Answer

The two World Wars of the 20th century—World War I (1914–1918) and World War II (1939–1945)—were cataclysmic events driven by long-standing tensions, imperial ambitions, militarism, nationalism, and complex international alliances [SRC-22b60bd3a819].

### Causes and Context
- **Underlying Factors:** European powers competed for resources and dominance through imperialism in Africa, Asia, and the Pacific [SRC-22b60bd3a819].
This colonial rivalry, combined with intense nationalism and militarism (the glorification of military power and arms buildups), fueled an environment ripe for conflict [SRC-22b60bd3a819].
- **World War I Aftermath:** WWI led to the dissolution of major empires (the Austro-Hungarian, Ottoman, Russian, and German empires) and gave birth to international organizations like the League of Nations [SRC-f71af10087db].
However, the harsh reparations imposed on Germany by the Treaty of Versailles fostered deep resentment and contributed to the rise of extremist ideologies that triggered the second global conflict. [SRC-f71af10087db]
- **World War II Scale:** Spanning from September 1939 to September 1945, World War II involved over 30 nations and resulted in an estimated 70 to 85 million deaths, making it the deadliest event in human history [SRC-08a292bdc706].
For the first time, civilian casualties significantly outnumbered military deaths due to total war tactics, genocide (such as the Holocaust), strategic bombings, and man-made famines [SRC-08a292bdc706].

### Global Impacts and Outcomes
- **Political Restructuring:** The defeat of the Axis powers (Germany, Italy, and Japan) led to the dismantling of their totalitarian regimes and the establishment of democratic systems [SRC-f71af10087db].
- **Multilateralism:** While the League of Nations ultimately failed to prevent WWII, it laid foundational principles of collective security and multilateral cooperation that were later realized more effectively through organizations established after the second conflict [SRC-f71af10087db].
