## Answer

A supercomputer is a computing system of exceptional power capable of performing huge volumes of operations per second, representing the pinnacle of computing performance [SRC-2e85d1690d1f].
Unlike typical desktop PCs or servers, a supercomputer is often room-sized, packing millions of processor cores and achieving speeds millions of times faster than standard personal computers to tackle the world's most complex scientific problems [SRC-c49e2c3f33d6].

### How Supercomputers Work
- **Parallel Processing:** Instead of relying on a single processor to execute instructions sequentially, supercomputers use parallel processing, clusters, and massively parallel architectures to divide massive problems into smaller chunks and solve them simultaneously (unverified: no matching retrieved evidence).
- **Processor Cores:** Modern supercomputers utilize immense hardware scaling; for example, the Frontier system at Oak Ridge National Laboratory features approximately 8,730,112 processor cores [SRC-c49e2c3f33d6].

### Performance Metrics (FLOPS)
Supercomputer performance is measured in **FLOPS** (Floating Point Operations Per Second), distinguishing them from ordinary computers [SRC-2e85d1690d1f].
- Typical modern desktop computers or servers operate in **gigaflops** (GFLOPS) or **teraflops** (TFLOPS) [SRC-2e85d1690d1f].
- Modern supercomputers reach scales of **petaflops** (PFLOPS) and **exaflops** (EFLOPS) [SRC-2e85d1690d1f].
For instance, Frontier set a performance record of 1.1 exaflops (1.1 million million million FLOPS) in 2022 [SRC-c49e2c3f33d6].

### Primary Applications and Use Cases
Supercomputers are deployed across industries requiring maximum computing power and big data analysis [SRC-2e85d1690d1f]:
- **Scientific Research & Modeling:** Used for high-energy physics, astrophysics (such as black hole merger simulations), and origin-of-the-universe models [SRC-2e85d1690d1f].
- **Weather and Climate Forecasting:** Meteorologists model oceans and the atmosphere, predicting hurricanes, cataclysms, and decades-long climate shifts [SRC-2e85d1690d1f].
- **Biology, Medicine, and Drug Discovery:** Systems like Fugaku aid in molecular research and drug development [SRC-f5a5e6325858].
- **National Security & Artificial Intelligence:** Systems like Summit support AI research and genomics [SRC-f5a5e6325858], while Sierra handles simulations for nuclear stockpile management [SRC-f5a5e6325858].
Other systems like Sunway TaihuLight contribute to advanced manufacturing [SRC-f5a5e6325858] and Perlmutter supports astrophysics studies [SRC-f5a5e6325858].
