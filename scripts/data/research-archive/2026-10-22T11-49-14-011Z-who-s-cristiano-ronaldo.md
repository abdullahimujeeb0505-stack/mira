## Answer

Cristiano Ronaldo (Cristiano Ronaldo dos Santos Aveiro, born February 5, 1985) is a Portuguese professional footballer widely regarded as one of the greatest players in the history of the sport [SRC-8105d04ebc58].
He plays as a forward and is recognized for his goal-scoring prowess, physical strength, athleticism, and longevity [SRC-8105d04ebc58].

### Professional Career & Clubs
* **Sporting CP (2002–2003):** Began his professional senior career with 31 matches and 5 goals [SRC-ed19658c86e6].
* **Manchester United (2003–2009, 2021–2022):** Played two separate spells, making 292 appearances with 118 goals in his first stint and 54 appearances with 24 goals in his second (unverified: no matching retrieved evidence).
* **Real Madrid (2009–2018):** Achieved his most prolific spell at a single club, scoring 450 goals in 438 appearances and becoming the club's all-time record scorer [SRC-ed19658c86e6].
* **Juventus (2018–2021):** Scored 101 goals in 134 matches [SRC-ed19658c86e6].
* **Al-Nassr (2023–present):** Joined the Saudi Pro League club, where he has scored over 130 goals. [SRC-202626bc4ae8]

### International Career
Representing the Portugal national team since 2003, Ronaldo has earned over 230 caps and scored approximately 146 goals, making him the all-time leading goalscorer in men's international football [SRC-ed19658c86e6].
He captained Portugal to victory at the 2016 UEFA European Championship and the UEFA Nations League titles in 2019 and 2025 [SRC-8105d04ebc58].
Across his senior career for club and country, he has recorded nearly 980 official goals [SRC-ed19658c86e6].

### Major Records & Honors
* **Ballon d'Or Awards:** Won five times (2008, 2013, 2014, 2016, 2017) [SRC-ed19658c86e6].
* **UEFA Champions League:** The first player to win the competition five times, with records for most appearances (183) and most goals (140), alongside a total of 145 UEFA club competition goals [SRC-202626bc4ae8].
* **EURO Tournaments:** Holds records for the most EURO final tournaments (6), final tournament appearances (30), and final goals (14) [SRC-202626bc4ae8].
