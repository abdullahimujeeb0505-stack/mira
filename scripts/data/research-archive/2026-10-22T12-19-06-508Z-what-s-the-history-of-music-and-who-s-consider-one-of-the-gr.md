### History of Music: Major Eras

Music history spans from early oral traditions through various structured periods of artistic and cultural development [SRC-9e80d2559b42].
Major historical eras include: [SRC-9e80d2559b42]

* **Medieval Music (500–1400):** Spanning the Middle Ages, this era was primarily religious and composed for the Catholic Church, famously characterized by single-line unison Gregorian chants [SRC-49178ada5e17].
* **Renaissance Music (1400–1600):** A period of artistic and cultural growth defined by polyphony, which involved multiple independent melodies played or sung simultaneously [SRC-49178ada5e17].
* **Baroque Music (1600–1750):** Marked by continued cultural expansion and characterized by ornate melodies [SRC-49178ada5e17].
