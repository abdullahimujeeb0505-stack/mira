## Answer

Elon Musk (born June 28, 1971) is a South African-born entrepreneur, business executive, and the world's wealthiest individual [SRC-ae94cc7cd3f9].
He is known for driving technological innovation across multiple industries, including electric vehicles, private spaceflight, artificial intelligence, social media, and neural technology. [SRC-433f2c9fe37e]

### Early Life and Background

* **Early Aptitude:** Born in Pretoria, South Africa, Musk showed an early interest in computing and sold a video game he coded when he was 12 years old [SRC-ae94cc7cd3f9].
* **Education:** He attended the University of Pretoria briefly before moving to North America, where he earned degrees in economics and physics from the University of Pennsylvania [SRC-ae94cc7cd3f9].
He briefly enrolled in a graduate program at Stanford University before leaving after two days to pursue internet ventures [SRC-ae94cc7cd3f9].
* **Early Career Ventures:** Musk co-founded Zip2, a web software company acquired by Compaq in 1999, and later co-founded X.com, an online payment company that merged to become PayPal before being acquired by eBay in 2002. [SRC-3e2b7493f952]

### Primary Companies and Leadership Roles

Musk oversees a broad portfolio of companies spanning sustainable energy, aerospace, AI, and infrastructure [SRC-3e2b7493f952]:

* **Tesla, Inc.:** Musk joined Tesla in 2004 as an investor and board member, and has served as its CEO and product architect since October 2008, focusing on electric vehicles, energy storage, and solar products. [SRC-433f2c9fe37e]
* **SpaceX (Space Exploration Technologies Corporation):** Founded by Musk in 2002, he serves as the founder, CEO, and chief engineer. [SRC-433f2c9fe37e]
The company develops reusable rockets and spacecraft with the long-term goal of enabling human space travel and colonizing Mars. [SRC-433f2c9fe37e]
* **X (formerly Twitter):** Musk acquired the social media platform to manage its operations and public communications network. [SRC-ae94cc7cd3f9]
* **Neuralink:** A neurotechnology venture focused on developing implantable brain-computer interfaces to help restore communication abilities to non-verbal or paralyzed individuals (unverified: no matching retrieved evidence).
* **xAI:** An artificial intelligence company founded in 2023 to advance AI capabilities, which sources note was acquired by SpaceX in 2026 [SRC-3e2b7493f952].
* **The Boring Company:** An infrastructure and tunnel construction company. [SRC-ae94cc7cd3f9]

### Global Impact

Musk is recognized as a major force in modern technology, having shifted global automotive markets toward electric vehicles and commercialized private space exploration. [SRC-3e2b7493f952]
