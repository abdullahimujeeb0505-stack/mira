**First orbital launch (2008)** – SpaceX’s Falcon 1 became the first privately‑developed liquid‑fuel rocket to reach orbit, proving the viability of commercial space launch (Source: SpaceX History: A Complete Timeline). [SRC-c63b9038b32f]

**Primary NASA crew‑transport provider (2020)** – SpaceX’s Crew Dragon replaced the retired Space Shuttle as the main vehicle for sending American astronauts to the International Space Station (Source: SpaceX History: A Complete Timeline). [SRC-c63b9038b32f]
