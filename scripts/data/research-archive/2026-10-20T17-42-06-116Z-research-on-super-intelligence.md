## Answer

**Definition**
Artificial superintelligence (ASI) is described as a hypothetical software‑based AI system whose intellectual capabilities exceed those of humans across all domains, possessing advanced cognitive functions and thinking skills beyond any human capacity. [SRC-e4fa914d10f5]
The IBM source also notes that ASI is still speculative and that its feasibility is debated, with some thinkers questioning whether human intelligence is an optimal template for replication. [SRC-e4fa914d10f5]

**Theoretical Foundations**
The concept builds on the progression from narrow AI (ANI) – systems that excel at specific tasks – to artificial general intelligence (AGI), which can learn and reason broadly like a human. [SRC-e4fa914d10f5]
Once AGI is achieved, the theory holds that it could engage in recursive self‑improvement, rapidly surpassing human limits and leading to ASI (unverified: no matching retrieved evidence).

*Collective Intelligence* – Enhancing global collaboration through interconnected platforms may amplify human and machine problem‑solving, potentially forming a hybrid intelligence that accelerates the transition to ASI. [SRC-72c111ffb510]
