## Answer

Cristiano Ronaldo (full name Cristiano Ronaldo dos Santos Aveiro, born February 5, 1985) is a Portuguese professional footballer who plays as a forward. [SRC-83c65cc45d4b]
Widely recognized as one of the greatest and most successful players in football history, he is known for his remarkable goal-scoring ability, physical strength, and longevity. [SRC-83c65cc45d4b]

### Career Milestones
- **Club Career:** Began his professional career at Sporting CP in 2002 before moving on to Manchester United, Real Madrid (where he became the all-time leading goalscorer with 450 goals in 438 appearances), Juventus, and Saudi Arabian club Al Nassr. [SRC-83c65cc45d4b]
His contract with Al Nassr runs through 2027 [SRC-83c65cc45d4b].
- **International Career:** Captains the Portugal national team, winning the UEFA European Championship in 2016 and the UEFA Nations League titles in 2019 and 2025. [SRC-83c65cc45d4b]

### Major Achievements
- **Individual Awards:** Won five Ballon d'Or titles. [SRC-83c65cc45d4b]
- **Club Honors:** Secured five UEFA Champions League titles alongside numerous domestic honors. [SRC-83c65cc45d4b]

### Cultural Impact
Beyond his athletic achievements, Ronaldo is a globally recognized sports personality, entrepreneur, brand ambassador, and philanthropist, making him one of the most influential athletes of the modern era [SRC-8105d04ebc58].
