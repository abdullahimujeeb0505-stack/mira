## Answer

Cristiano Ronaldo dos Santos Aveiro (born February 5, 1985) is a Portuguese professional footballer who plays as a forward and is widely recognized as one of the most successful players in football history [SRC-83c65cc45d4b].

### Career Milestones
- **Club Career:** He began his professional career at Sporting CP in 2002 before moving on to Manchester United, Real Madrid (where he became the all-time leading goalscorer with 450 goals in 438 appearances), Juventus, and Saudi Arabian club Al Nassr, with a contract running through 2027 [SRC-83c65cc45d4b].
- **International Career:** He captains the Portugal national team, winning the UEFA European Championship in 2016 and UEFA Nations League titles in 2019 and 2025 [SRC-83c65cc45d4b].

### Major Achievements
- **Individual Awards:** Secured five Ballon d'Or titles [SRC-83c65cc45d4b].
- **Club Honors:** Secured five UEFA Champions League titles alongside multiple domestic honors [SRC-83c65cc45d4b].
