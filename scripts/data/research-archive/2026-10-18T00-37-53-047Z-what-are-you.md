## Answer

ChatGPT is an artificial‑intelligence chatbot created by OpenAI that is built on a large language model (LLM) architecture. [SRC-a4529e419022]
It uses a transformer‑based neural network that processes text by tokenizing input, converting tokens to embeddings, applying self‑attention across multiple transformer layers, and predicting the next token to generate coherent, human‑like responses (unverified: no matching retrieved evidence).
As an LLM, ChatGPT learns statistical and semantic patterns from massive text corpora, enabling it to answer questions, write content, translate languages, and perform many other natural‑language tasks [SRC-a4529e419022].
