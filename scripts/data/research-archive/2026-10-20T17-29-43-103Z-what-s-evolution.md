## Answer

Definition of Evolution: [SRC-7877a0768741]
Evolution is the process by which biological populations undergo changes in their inherited traits over successive generations, driven by the gradual accumulation of genetic variations. [SRC-7877a0768741]
These changes can lead to the emergence of new traits or species. [SRC-7877a0768741]

Core Principles: [SRC-7877a0768741]
- Genetic variation arises through mutation, migration, and other mechanisms and provides the raw material for evolution. [SRC-4a51cb74998c]
- Natural selection acts on this variation: individuals with traits better suited to their environment tend to survive and reproduce more, making those traits more common in the population. [SRC-7877a0768741]
- Evolution requires a change in gene (allele) frequencies within a population over time; such changes are heritable and passed to the next generation. [SRC-4a51cb74998c]
- Other mechanisms, such as genetic drift (random changes in small populations) and gene flow (movement of genes between populations), also contribute to evolutionary change. [SRC-4a51cb74998c]
