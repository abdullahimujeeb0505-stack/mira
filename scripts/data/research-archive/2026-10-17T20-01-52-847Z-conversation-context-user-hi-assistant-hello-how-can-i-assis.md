## Answer

**Verified milestones**
- **Initial announcement & first public release**: TypeScript was introduced by Microsoft’s Anders Hejlsberg and the first public version (0.8) was released in **October 2012**【1†L1-L4】【2†L9-L13】.
- **Early version series**: The version table lists the progression from 0.8 (Oct 2012) through the pre‑2.0 era (0.8 – 1.8, 2012‑2016)【1†L7-L10】.
- **TypeScript 2.0**: Marked the start of the “Inference” era and was released in **2016** (the era spans 2.0 – 3.9, 2016‑2020)【1†L12-L13】.
- **TypeScript 3.x**: Continued the inference era (2016‑2020) with features such as mapped types and conditional types【1†L12-L13】.
- **TypeScript 4.0**: Begins the “Modern” era from **2020 onward**, introducing template‑literal types and other modern tooling features【1†L13-L14】.
- **TypeScript 7.0**: The latest listed release shipped on **8 July 2026**, featuring a Go‑native compiler and major performance gains【1†L20-L23】.
