## Answer

### Background
Burna Boy (born Damini Ebunoluwa Ogulu in 1991 in Port Harcourt, Rivers State, Nigeria) is a singer, songwriter, and record producer [SRC-face6e2e30b0].
He rose to stardom in 2012 following the release of "Like to Party," the lead single from his debut studio album, *L.I.F.E.* [SRC-face6e2e30b0].
His artistic lineage includes a direct connection to the origins of Afrobeat, as his grandfather once managed Nigerian icon Fela Kuti [SRC-8daf8e0406ac].

### Musical Style and Impact
Burna Boy's music blends African rhythms, reggae, dancehall, R&B, and hip-hop. [SRC-face6e2e30b0]
While he is widely recognized as a defining global voice of the contemporary African genre Afrobeats, he has frequently used the term "Afrofusion" to describe his distinct mix of sounds and cultures. [SRC-face6e2e30b0]
However, his claims of having personally created the genre "Afrofusion" have been noted as a subject of debate and critical analysis by music scholars [SRC-face6e2e30b0].

### Major Achievements and Awards
- **Grammy Success**: Burna Boy has earned extensive recognition from the Recording Academy, accumulating 13 Grammy nominations and 1 win [SRC-8daf8e0406ac].
His major projects, including *African Giant*, *Twice as Tall*, *Love, Damini*, and *I Told Them…*, have received widespread critical acclaim. [SRC-8daf8e0406ac]
- **Historic Wins**: He won a Grammy Award for his album *Twice as Tall*, marking a landmark moment for contemporary African music. [SRC-face6e2e30b0]
- **Global Touring**: He achieved milestone status by becoming the first African artist to headline and sell out major stadium and arena venues in the United States and Europe, such as Madison Square Garden. [SRC-8daf8e0406ac]
