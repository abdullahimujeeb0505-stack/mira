## Answer

### Overview and Origin of Action Research
Action research is a philosophy and methodology of research applied in the social sciences, education, and organizational contexts. [SRC-a883f536f6c5]
It seeks transformative change through the simultaneous process of taking action and conducting research, which are linked by critical reflection [SRC-a883f536f6c5].
The term was coined in 1944 by Kurt Lewin, a German-American psychologist at MIT, who elaborated on the concept in his 1946 paper "Action Research and Minority Problems". [SRC-a883f536f6c5]
Lewin's purpose was to ensure that inquiry drives real, lasting social change simultaneously with academic understanding rather than merely producing isolated academic papers [SRC-5d4a0709d4c2].

### Core Stages of the Action Research Cycle
Action research utilizes an interactive inquiry process structured around a repeating cycle of steps: [SRC-a883f536f6c5]
* **Planning:** Researchers and practitioners collaborate to diagnose a real, local problem and plan an intervention or change intended to address it [SRC-c0013bfb7930].
In Lewin's formulation, this involves a spiral of steps utilizing a circle of planning, action, and fact-finding [SRC-a883f536f6c5].
* **Action:** Implementing the planned problem-solving action within a collaborative setting (unverified: no matching retrieved evidence).
* **Observation (Fact-finding):** Systematically studying and observing the effects, conditions, and results of the implemented action. [SRC-a883f536f6c5]
* **Reflection / Analysis:** Engaging in data-driven collaborative analysis and critical reflection to understand underlying causes and outcomes, which informs future predictions and revisions. [SRC-a883f536f6c5]

### Practical Application and Iterative Process
In practice, action research operates as an iterative inquiry process where participants jointly plan, implement, and study a change, then revise and repeat the cycle based on what was learned [SRC-c0013bfb7930].
Unlike traditional research designs that aim only to describe or explain a phenomenon, action research explicitly strives to produce practical improvements in specific settings while generating transferable knowledge [SRC-c0013bfb7930].
Over decades of development, methods have evolved to balance focus between the actions taken and the research resulting from reflective understanding [SRC-a883f536f6c5].
