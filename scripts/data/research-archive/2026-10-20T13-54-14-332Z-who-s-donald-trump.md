Donald J. [SRC-8a4fbbe1c86e]
Trump is an American businessman, media figure, and politician who served as the 45th President of the United States (2017‑2021). [SRC-8a4fbbe1c86e]

**Early life and education** [SRC-6cff389065ce]
- Born June 14, 1946, in Queens, New York, the fourth of five children of real‑estate developer Fred Trump [SRC-6cff389065ce][SRC-11a85b574ed3].
- Attended Fordham University (1964) before transferring to the Wharton School of the University of Pennsylvania, earning a B.S. in economics in May 1968 [SRC-11a85b574ed3].

**Business career** [SRC-6cff389065ce]
- Joined his father’s firm after graduation and helped expand it into Manhattan, eventually rebranding it as the Trump Organization, known for projects such as Trump Tower, hotels, casinos, and golf resorts [SRC-6cff389065ce].
- Authored more than fourteen best‑selling books, including *The Art of the Deal* (considered a business classic) [SRC-8a4fbbe1c86e].
