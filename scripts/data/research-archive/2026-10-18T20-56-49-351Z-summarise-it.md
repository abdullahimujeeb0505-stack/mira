## Answer

NICE develops evidence summaries in line with its equality and diversity scheme and declaration of interests policy. [SRC-7f13774400ce]
The standard process usually takes about 12 weeks, though a longer period may be allowed for large evidence volumes, and a rapid evidence summary can be produced more quickly by omitting or shortening some steps, with the resource impact assessment omitted at minimum [SRC-7f13774400ce].

Key steps in the development process are: [SRC-7f13774400ce]
1.
Define and agree the final scope using the PICO framework with clinical advisers, external experts and commissioners. [SRC-7f13774400ce]
2.
Send a search request to information services. [SRC-7f13774400ce]
3.
Contact the relevant company for data, if applicable. [SRC-7f13774400ce]
4.
Conduct the literature search. [SRC-7f13774400ce]
5.
Sift and select the evidence. [SRC-7f13774400ce]
6.
Appraise and summarise the evidence. [SRC-7f13774400ce]
7.
Produce an initial draft. [SRC-7f13774400ce]
8.
Perform an internal technical check of the revised draft. [SRC-7f13774400ce]
9.
Distribute the revised draft to the company (if applicable), external experts, targeted stakeholders, relevant NICE teams and the Medicines and Healthcare products Regulatory Agency (MHRA) for review. [SRC-7f13774400ce]
10.
Incorporate review comments and produce revised drafts for final sign‑off [SRC-7f13774400ce].
