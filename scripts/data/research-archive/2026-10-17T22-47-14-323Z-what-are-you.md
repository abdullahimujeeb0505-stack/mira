## Answer

**ChatGPT – What it is** [SRC-23f46a6ed620]
ChatGPT is a generative AI chatbot developed by OpenAI. [SRC-23f46a6ed620]
It runs on OpenAI’s proprietary GPT (Generative Pre‑trained Transformer) family of large language models, most recently the multimodal GPT‑4o model, which allows it to handle text, images and audio inputs[SRC-23f46a6ed620].
As a large language model (LLM), ChatGPT predicts the next word or token in a sequence, enabling it to produce human‑like text responses[SRC-65d77a277b29].

**Creator**
ChatGPT was created by OpenAI, a machine‑learning startup founded by Sam Altman, Greg Brockman, Elon Musk, Ilya Sutskever and others[SRC-23f46a6ed620].

**Primary functions**
- Hold lifelike, natural‑language conversations with users. [SRC-23f46a6ed620]
- Generate various forms of content: articles, summaries, advice, code, and other text‑based outputs. [SRC-23f46a6ed620]
- With the multimodal GPT‑4o engine, also create images and engage in audio‑based interactions[SRC-23f46a6ed620].
- Serve as a reasoning and analysis tool, leveraging the capabilities of its underlying GPT‑4 model[SRC-fab57e020b0c].

In summary, ChatGPT is an OpenAI‑built AI chatbot that leverages a large language model (GPT‑4o) to understand and generate natural language (and multimodal) content for conversational and content‑creation tasks. [SRC-23f46a6ed620]
