### Time-of-Use (TOU) Rates and Tariffs
* **Definition:** A TOU rate or tariff is an electricity pricing structure that varies the cost of electricity depending on the time of day it is used, in contrast to traditional flat-rate pricing [SRC-cc2d0a49e9fe], [SRC-0424700fe80b].
* **Pricing Structure:** Rates are divided into specific time periods. [SRC-cc2d0a49e9fe]
Peak demand periods (such as late afternoons and early evenings) carry higher rates, whereas off-peak periods (such as late at night or early morning) offer lower rates [SRC-cc2d0a49e9fe].
* **Purpose:** TOU pricing incentivizes consumers to shift power consumption to low-demand periods, helping reduce electricity bills, lower strain on the electric grid, and support environmental sustainability and renewable energy integration [SRC-cc2d0a49e9fe].
