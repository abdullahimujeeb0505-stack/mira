## Answer

You are interacting with OpenAI’s ChatGPT product, which as of October 2026 is powered by the new GPT‑6 model. [SRC-04e436bc7910]
OpenAI rolled out GPT‑6 Sol for Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise subscribers and GPT‑6 Luna for Free/Go users, both delivering the Interactive UI experience (e.g., charts, forms, and live‑thinking responses)[SRC-04e436bc7910][SRC-e0088f49f35b].
