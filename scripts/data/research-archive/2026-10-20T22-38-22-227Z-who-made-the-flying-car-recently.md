## Answer

The US startup Alef Aeronautics developed and recently tested a flying car prototype known as the Model Zero [SRC-405973c322a5].

### Project Details
- **Developer**: Alef Aeronautics (founded in 2015, led by CEO Jim Dukhovny) [SRC-405973c322a5].
- **Vehicle Prototype**: Model Zero (with a commercial Model A vehicle also in development) [SRC-405973c322a5].
- **Design & Performance**: Unlike traditional quadcopter drones or folding-wing designs, the Alef vehicle features a more conventional automotive design with rotor blades hidden inside the chassis [SRC-405973c322a5].
It is 100% electric, with a reported driving range of 320 kilometers and a flight range of approximately 160 kilometers [SRC-405973c322a5].
- **Status**: Alef Aeronautics demonstrated a public flight test in California where the Model Zero prototype hopped over a parked car on a public road [SRC-405973c322a5].
The company has secured over 3,300 pre-orders for its Model A vehicle, reached a manufacturing agreement with PUCARA Aero and MYC, and anticipated entering production [SRC-405973c322a5].
