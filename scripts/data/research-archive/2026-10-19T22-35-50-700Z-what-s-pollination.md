## Research plan

Provide a clear, concise definition of pollination, including the basic mechanisms, primary agents, and its ecological significance.


## Answer

Pollination is the transfer of pollen grains from the male anther of a flower to the female stigma of the same or another flower, enabling fertilization and seed production [SRC-0863fbe7995e].
It occurs via biotic agents such as insects (bees, butterflies, moths, flies, beetles), birds, and bats, as well as abiotic agents like wind and water [SRC-0863fbe7995e]; animal‑mediated pollination alone supports the reproduction of most flowering plants and about 80% of global crops, providing essential ecosystem services and food production.
