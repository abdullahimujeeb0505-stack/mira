## Answer

ChatGPT was created by OpenAI, the artificial‑intelligence research organization founded in December 2015 by Sam Altman, Elon Musk, Ilya Sutskever, Greg Brockman and others. [SRC-0af955c6b8fe]
OpenAI developed and released the ChatGPT product on 30 November 2022 [SRC-0af955c6b8fe].
