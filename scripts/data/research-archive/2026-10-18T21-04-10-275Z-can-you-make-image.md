## Research plan

Determine whether the current ChatGPT model can generate images, and under what conditions or tools this capability is available.

- Can ChatGPT make or generate images?

## Answer

Yes.
The current ChatGPT product includes an image‑generation feature called **GPT Images 2.5** that can create pictures directly in the chat interface (unverified: no matching retrieved evidence).
According to the OpenAI Developer Community announcement, GPT Images 2.5 is rolling out to *all* ChatGPT, ChatGPT Work, and Codex users on desktop, mobile, and web as of September 2026, providing faster, higher‑fidelity images and tools such as Sketch and template‑based creation [SRC-83ddc97f8b94].

Before GPT Images 2.5, image creation was provided through **DALL·E 3** and the **GPT‑4o** multimodal model. [SRC-83ddc97f8b94]
The GeeksforGeeks guide notes that using DALL·E 3 inside ChatGPT required selecting the GPT‑4 model and a “DALL·E (beta)” option, and that a ChatGPT Plus subscription (i.e., a paid tier) was needed to access it [SRC-c4ef345e2c07].
The LinkedIn article on GPT‑4o explains that, after its launch in March 2025, image generation with GPT‑4o was restricted to ChatGPT Plus, Pro, and Team subscribers, while free‑tier users were limited to the older DALL·E 3 system with a cap of two images per day [SRC-d553e8150aa4].

**Key conditions and limits**
- **Access tier**: As of the September 2026 rollout, GPT Images 2.5 is available to all users regardless of subscription level (unverified: no matching retrieved evidence).
Earlier versions (DALL·E 3, GPT‑4o) required a paid subscription for the most advanced capabilities and imposed daily image caps for free users (unverified: no matching retrieved evidence).
- **How to generate**: In the current UI, users can simply type a prompt; the system will automatically produce images, or they can invoke tools like “@ Sketch” for drawing assistance (as described in the announcement) (unverified: no matching retrieved evidence).
For DALL·E 3, users needed to select GPT‑4, choose the DALL·E (beta) option, enter a prompt, and then download the result. [SRC-c4ef345e2c07]
- **Content policies**: All image generation follows OpenAI’s usage policies, which prohibit disallowed content (e.g., violent or sexual imagery involving minors) and enforce safety filters, though the specific policy details are not enumerated in the supplied sources (unverified: no matching retrieved evidence).
- **Performance**: GPT Images 2.5 promises improved fidelity, consistent detail across edits, and faster generation, but the exact token cost or usage quotas for free users were not disclosed in the available evidence. [SRC-83ddc97f8b94]

In summary, ChatGPT can generate images, and as of the latest announced feature (GPT Images 2.5) this capability is broadly available to all users, superseding earlier, subscription‑restricted options like DALL·E 3 and GPT‑4o (unverified: no matching retrieved evidence).
