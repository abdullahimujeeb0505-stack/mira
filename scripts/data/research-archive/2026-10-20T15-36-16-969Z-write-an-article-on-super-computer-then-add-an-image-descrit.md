## Answer

Supercomputers are the most powerful computing systems available at a given time, built to perform vastly more floating‑point operations per second than ordinary servers or workstations. [SRC-3e6b1c9ef2e7]
Their defining trait is not a fixed FLOPS threshold but their position at the frontier of sustained computational capability, which shifts as technology advances [SRC-3e6b1c9ef2e7].

**Core Architectural Components**
- **Processors and Accelerators**: Modern systems combine many CPU cores with GPUs or other accelerators, which deliver far higher FLOPS per watt for dense matrix workloads [SRC-3e6b1c9ef2e7].
- **Interconnects**: High‑bandwidth, low‑latency networks such as InfiniBand or custom fabrics link thousands of nodes, enabling the massive parallelism required for scientific codes [SRC-3e6b1c9ef2e7].
