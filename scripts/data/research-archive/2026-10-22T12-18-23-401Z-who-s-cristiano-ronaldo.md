### Overview
Cristiano Ronaldo dos Santos Aveiro is a Portuguese professional footballer born on February 5, 1985, in Funchal, Madeira, Portugal [SRC-7d4fa04b7783].
He is widely recognized as one of the greatest players in the history of the sport, celebrated for his exceptional goal-scoring ability, athleticism, speed, and longevity [SRC-7d4fa04b7783].

### Major Career Milestones and Club Statistics
Ronaldo has played for several prominent European clubs and the Saudi Pro League, accumulating a career total of 980 official goals in 1,331 matches as of October 2026. [SRC-7d4fa04b7783]

* **Sporting CP (2002–2003):** Began his professional senior career with 31 matches and 5 goals [SRC-ed19658c86e6].
* **Manchester United (2003–2009, 2021–2022):** Played two spells with the English club, recording 292 matches with 118 goals in his first stint and 54 matches with 24 goals in his second stint [SRC-ed19658c86e6].
During his early years under manager Sir Alex Ferguson, he won three consecutive Premier League titles and a UEFA Champions League title [SRC-7d4fa04b7783].
* **Real Madrid (2009–2018):** Achieved his most prolific club spell, scoring 450 goals in 438 appearances and remaining the club's all-time record scorer [SRC-ed19658c86e6].
* **Juventus (2018–2021):** Contributed 101 goals across 134 matches [SRC-ed19658c86e6].
* **Al-Nassr (2023–Present):** Competes in the Saudi Pro League, where he has scored roughly 136 goals in approximately 148 matches. [SRC-7d4fa04b7783]

### International Career and Honors
Representing the Portugal national team since 2003, Ronaldo has earned approximately 234 caps and scored roughly 146 international goals, making him the all-time leading goalscorer in men's international football [SRC-ed19658c86e6].
He captained Portugal to victory at the UEFA European Championship in 2016 [SRC-7d4fa04b7783].
