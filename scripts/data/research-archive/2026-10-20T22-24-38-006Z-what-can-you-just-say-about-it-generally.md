### Overview of Photon (photon.codes)

Photon is a San Francisco-based startup and messaging platform designed for AI agents. [SRC-258c7044c7cb]
Founded by Daniel Tian and Ryan Zhu, the platform provides an open-source framework and toolkit (such as Spectrum) that helps developers wire AI agents directly into everyday messaging channels like iMessage, WhatsApp, Telegram, and Slack. [SRC-258c7044c7cb]
Its primary mission is summarized by its developer portal tagline: "Bring your agents to millions of lives" [SRC-8de2ed019f6a].

### Target Audience and Purpose

* **Developers and Startups:** Aimed at builders looking to bypass the friction of traditional user acquisition, such as getting users to download separate mobile applications or visit new browser tabs (unverified: no matching retrieved evidence).
* **Conversational Distribution:** Focuses on meeting users where they already spend time, leveraging platforms that boast billions of active participants (e.g., WhatsApp and iMessage). [SRC-fd1bac6793da]

### Primary Functions and Features

* **Multi-Channel Messaging Infrastructure:** Manages identity, state, deliverability, and analytics across distinct communication layers [SRC-258c7044c7cb].
* **Rich Interactive Capabilities:** Connects agents to native chat features like group chats, tapbacks, reply threads, rich media, polls, message effects, live location, and mini-apps (such as WhatsApp Flows or iMessage apps) [SRC-258c7044c7cb].
* **Developer Portal:** Provides a centralized dashboard (`app.photon.codes`) featuring authentication via email or Google to help developers manage applications and agent deployments (unverified: no matching retrieved evidence).
