## Answer

ChatGPT is an AI conversational system created by OpenAI [SRC-183d629cd30a].
It is delivered as a product that leverages OpenAI’s family of large‑language models (LLMs), the most recent of which is the GPT‑6 series released in September 2026 [SRC-183d629cd30a].
Like other modern LLMs, ChatGPT operates on a transformer architecture: input text is tokenized, each token is turned into an embedding vector, the embeddings pass through multiple self‑attention‑based transformer blocks, and the model predicts the next token to generate fluent, context‑aware responses [SRC-a4529e419022].
The system’s purpose is to help users obtain information, generate creative content, translate language, and solve problems through natural‑language interaction (unverified: no matching retrieved evidence).
Earlier technical documentation describes the GPT‑4 model, which previously powered ChatGPT and outlines its training and capabilities (unverified: no matching retrieved evidence).
