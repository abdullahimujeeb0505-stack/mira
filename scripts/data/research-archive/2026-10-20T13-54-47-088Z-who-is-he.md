## Answer

Donald J. [SRC-8a4fbbe1c86e]
Trump is an American businessman, media personality, and politician who served as the 45th President of the United States from 2017 to 2021. [SRC-8a4fbbe1c86e]
Born on June 14, 1946, in Queens, New York, he graduated from the Wharton School of Finance at the University of Pennsylvania and took over his father’s real‑estate firm in 1971, renaming it the Trump Organization, which expanded into hotels, resorts, casinos, and golf courses [SRC-09bec8b731ba].
He authored more than fourteen bestseller books, beginning with *The Art of the Deal* (1987), and launched the reality‑TV series *The Apprentice* in 2004, cementing his public‑media profile [SRC-09bec8b731ba].
After winning the Republican nomination in 2016, he defeated Hillary Clinton in the Electoral College and served one term, during which he signed a major tax reform bill and pursued protectionist trade policies [SRC-09bec8b731ba][SRC-8a4fbbe1c86e].
