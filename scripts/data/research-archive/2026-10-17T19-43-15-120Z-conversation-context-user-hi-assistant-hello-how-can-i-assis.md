## Answer

Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants, algae, and some bacteria use sunlight to turn water and carbon dioxide into a sugar (glucose) and oxygen, providing food and breathable air for most life on Earth [1].

**Key facts**
- Chlorophyll inside chloroplasts captures light energy, especially red and blue wavelengths, to power the reactions [2].
- The process occurs in two stages: light‑dependent reactions (in the thylakoid membranes) split water and generate energy carriers ATP and NADPH, followed by the Calvin cycle (light‑independent reactions in the stroma) that uses those carriers to convert carbon dioxide into glucose [1].
- Photosynthesis produces the oxygen we breathe and forms the base of most food chains on the planet [1].
