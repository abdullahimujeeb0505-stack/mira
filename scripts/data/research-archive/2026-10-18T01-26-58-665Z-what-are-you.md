## Answer

ChatGPT is a conversational artificial‑intelligence chatbot created by OpenAI. [SRC-d9222d35d6f3]
It runs on OpenAI’s Generative Pre‑trained Transformer (GPT) family of large language models, which use deep‑learning and natural‑language‑processing techniques to generate human‑like text and, in its latest multimodal version (GPT‑4o), also images and audio [SRC-23f46a6ed620].
The service was first released in 2022 and is designed to let users “talk to AI and have AI talk back,” providing answers, drafting content, writing code, creating images, summarizing documents, translating language, and more (unverified: no matching retrieved evidence).
OpenAI, the AI research organization founded by Sam Altman, Greg Brockman and others, develops and maintains ChatGPT as part of its broader suite of generative AI products [SRC-23f46a6ed620].
