## Research plan

Provide a clear, concise definition of education, including its core components and commonly accepted scholarly perspectives.


## Answer

**Definition** [SRC-fcb91dd30cf1]
Education is the process of acquiring knowledge, skills, values, and attitudes through various forms of learning. [SRC-fcb91dd30cf1]
It includes formal instruction in schools and universities as well as non‑formal and informal learning such as community engagement and self‑directed study. [SRC-fcb91dd30cf1]

**Core Components**
- **Knowledge and Skills**: Cognitive content and practical abilities that learners develop (unverified: no matching retrieved evidence).
- **Values and Attitudes**: Norms, ethics, and dispositions shaped through education. [SRC-fcb91dd30cf1]
- **Learning Contexts**:
- *Formal*: Structured curricula in early years, schools, higher and further education institutions. [SRC-a890bf972f04]
 - *Non‑formal*: Organized programs outside the formal system (e.g., community courses) (unverified: no matching retrieved evidence).
- *Informal*: Everyday experiences, self‑directed study, and social interaction (unverified: no matching retrieved evidence).

**Scholarly Perspectives**
Education research treats the field as an integration of theory, research, and practice. [SRC-4e601ca72d2b]
Textbooks such as the *Core Concepts in Higher Education* series emphasize critical inquiry, evidence‑based frameworks, and the need to address systemic challenges, reflecting a scholarly view that education is both a discipline of study and a professional practice. [SRC-4e601ca72d2b]
