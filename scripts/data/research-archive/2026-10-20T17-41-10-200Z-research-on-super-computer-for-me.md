Supercomputers are specialized machines built to perform complex calculations at speeds far beyond ordinary computers. [SRC-ac86da72d8c5]
Unlike typical desktops that rely on a single or few CPUs, a supercomputer integrates **thousands of processors** working in parallel, often combined with accelerators such as GPUs, and is equipped with massive memory and high‑bandwidth interconnect fabrics to move data quickly across nodes.
