## Answer

Donald John Trump was born on June 14, 1946, in Queens, New York, as the fourth of five children of real estate developer Fred Trump [SRC-6cff389065ce].

### Business Career
- After graduating from business school in 1968, Trump joined his father's company, Trump Management [SRC-6cff389065ce].
- He expanded the family enterprise into Manhattan, rebranding it as the Trump Organization, and focused on high-profile developments such as luxury hotels, casinos, golf resorts, and Trump Tower [SRC-6cff389065ce].

### Political Career and Presidencies
- **First Presidency (2017–2021):** Trump won the November 8, 2016, presidential election as the Republican nominee and was inaugurated as the 45th President of the United States on January 20, 2017 [SRC-4deff56b86f5].
He was the first president with no prior public office or military leadership experience [SRC-4deff56b86f5].
His first term concluded after the 2020 election [SRC-4deff56b86f5].
- **Second Presidency (2025–present):** Following a victory in the 2024 election, Trump became the second person in U.S. history—and the first since the 19th century—to be elected to two non-consecutive presidential terms, taking the oath of office for his second term on January 20, 2025 [SRC-4deff56b86f5].
